Courrier

A desktop mail client for macOS that shows several Gmail and Google Workspace accounts in one inbox, with a chat-style reply composer.

Courrier is a personal project by Pierre-Luc Bacon. It runs on the developer's own Mac and is not distributed to other people.

What it does with your Google account

Courrier signs in with Google and asks for a single permission, gmail.modify. It uses it to:

  • list and read the conversations in your inbox;
  • mark messages read or unread, and archive them;
  • send the replies and messages you write;
  • read your Gmail send-as addresses, to choose the From address.

This permission does not allow permanently deleting mail. Courrier does not ask for your contacts, calendar, Drive or any other Google data.

There is no Courrier server. The app on your Mac talks to Google directly, and your sign-in tokens stay between the two. Your mail goes elsewhere only when you use Courrier's Claude features, which send it to Anthropic through your own Claude Code login. Details are in the privacy policy.
