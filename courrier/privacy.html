Courrier privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

Courrier is a desktop mail client for macOS, written by Pierre-Luc Bacon for personal use. This page explains what it does with the data of the Google accounts you connect to it.

What Courrier accesses

When you add a Google account, Courrier asks Google for the gmail.modify permission. With it, Courrier reads the messages in your inbox and your Gmail send-as addresses, changes the read, unread and inbox labels of messages when you mark or archive them, and sends the messages you write. It cannot permanently delete mail, and it does not request any other Google data.

How it is used

Only to show your mail in the app and carry out what you do there. Courrier does not show ads, does not analyze your mail for any other purpose, and does not use it to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Where it is kept

  • Mail is downloaded from Gmail while the app runs and kept in memory. It is not saved to disk.
  • Replies you have started but not sent are saved on your Mac, in the app's local storage, until you send or discard them.
  • Google sign-in tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain.
  • The list of connected accounts (address, sidebar label, display name and color) is stored in ~/Library/Application Support/com.pierrelucbacon.courrier/.

Who it is shared with

No one. Courrier has no server: the app on your Mac talks only to Google (accounts.google.com, oauth2.googleapis.com and gmail.googleapis.com). The developer never receives your mail, tokens or any other data. There are no analytics, crash reports or other telemetry, and no data is sold or transferred to anyone.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Courrier's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

To revoke Courrier's access, open your Google account's third-party connections and remove Courrier. To delete what Courrier keeps on your Mac, delete the folder above and the Keychain items named com.pierrelucbacon.courrier.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above changed. Questions: pierrelucbacon@gmail.com.
