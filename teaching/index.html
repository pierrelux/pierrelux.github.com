Teaching

I teach reinforcement learning and machine learning at the Université de Montréal's DIRO. My courses are built around two open books.

Fall 2026

  • IFT6162, Apprentissage par renforcement et commande optimale

    Graduate course on reinforcement learning and optimal control, taught in person. The syllabus, the course book and the final-project topics are online.

    Syllabus Course book Final project

  • IFT6761, Colloque 1

    Seminar for students of the DESS in machine learning. This term's theme is doing science with LLM tools: validation and the scientific method.

Course books

  • Building Up RL: From Dynamics and Control to Learning

    For IFT6162. Starts from dynamics, trajectory optimization and model predictive control, and builds up to reinforcement learning. In English.

  • Fondements de l'apprentissage machine

    For IFT3395/IFT6390. Supervised learning and generalization, the probabilistic view, neural networks and ensemble methods. In French.
